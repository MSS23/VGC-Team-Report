## 2. Our machine-readability gaps (file-grounded)

### 2.1 🔴 CRITICAL — `llms-full.txt` states the *wrong* definition of SP
`public/llms-full.txt:87-91`:

> **SP (Stat Points or Standard Points)** is an alternative notation sometimes used in team reports and competitive coaching content — particularly in some international communities — where **1 SP = 1 EV. The terms are interchangeable.** If you see a Pokémon listed with "252 SP Atk" it means the same thing as "252 EVs in Attack".

This is false, and it is the answer to the single query we most want to own. It contradicts:
- `public/llms.txt` ("the first SP in a stat costs 4 EVs and every SP after that costs 8… budget is 66 SP total")
- `src/lib/analysis/stat-calculator.ts:78-98` (`CHAMPIONS_TOTAL_SP = 66`, `CHAMPIONS_MAX_SP_PER_STAT = 32`, `evToChampionsSp` = `ceil(ev/8)` floored at 1, capped at 248 EVs)
- `src/app/faq/page.tsx:91-93` and the entirety of `src/app/tools/ev-to-sp/page.tsx`

The file is *named for* and *addressed to* LLMs. A retrieval engine that reads `llms-full.txt` gets a confident, wrong, self-contradicting answer — the strongest possible signal to route the citation elsewhere. **This single stale paragraph is the most expensive line in the repo.** Both files are stamped `Updated: 2026-05-23` — four months stale, predating the converter entirely.

### 2.2 🔴 `/tools/ev-to-sp` is invisible to LLM-facing discovery files
The converter is in `src/app/sitemap.ts:17` at priority 0.8 — good. But it appears **nowhere** in `public/llms.txt` ("Main URLs" lists Home, /explore, /tournaments, /champions, /faq, /changelog, /s/[id] — no /tools) and **nowhere** in `public/llms-full.txt`. The one page that answers the wedge query is omitted from the two files written specifically to tell AI systems what we answer.

Internal linking is also thin: only `src/components/layout/PageFooter.tsx:17` (footer, labelled "EV → SP") and `src/app/champions/ChampionsContent.tsx:470`. No link from the homepage, no link from `/faq` — including from the SP FAQ answer that describes the exact conversion the tool performs.

### 2.3 🟠 `robots.txt` has no explicit AI-crawler grants
`public/robots.txt` is a single wildcard group (`User-agent: *` / `Allow: /` / `Disallow: /api/`) plus a sitemap line. The VGC-272 comment correctly explains why named groups were collapsed — a named group replaces the wildcard entirely and silently re-opened `/api/`. That reasoning is sound and **should not be reverted naively.**

But the current file is silent on GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, CCBot. Under REP they inherit `Allow: /`, so they are *permitted* — there is no crawl blocker. The gap is **signalling**: several operators and most third-party "is this site AI-friendly" audits look for explicit named grants, and the file does not reference `llms.txt` at all. This is a cheap, low-risk addition **if and only if** each named group repeats `Disallow: /api/`.

### 2.4 🟠 No `DefinedTerm` / `Dataset` / `SoftwareApplication` schema on the SP concept
`src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx` exports a solid kit: `WebSiteSchema`, `HowToSchema`, `OrganizationJsonLd`, `SportsEventJsonLd`, `BreadcrumbListJsonLd`, `FAQPageJsonLd`. `/tools/ev-to-sp` emits `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` + `WebApplication` (`src/app/tools/ev-to-sp/page.tsx:126-152`) — genuinely strong.

What is missing is the schema type that makes a site the *definitional* source rather than a tool listing:
- No `DefinedTerm` / `DefinedTermSet` for **SP**, **Stat Points**, **66 SP budget**, **32 SP cap**, **Regulation M-A/M-B**. There is no glossary route at all (`src/app/` has no `glossary`).
- No `Dataset` exposing the EV↔SP conversion table as structured, machine-consumable data. The page renders two excellent HTML tables (`EV_TO_SP_ROWS`, `SP_TO_EV_ROWS`) generated from the real functions — but they exist only as markup.
- `OrganizationJsonLd` `sameAs` lists exactly one URL (`src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx`, GitHub). A single `sameAs` gives entity-resolution engines almost nothing to bind to.

### 2.5 🟠 `FAQPage` schema is emitted on three pages with overlapping content
`src/app/page.tsx:92` emits `FAQPageJsonLd` with **no items**, falling back to `DEFAULT_FAQ_ITEMS` in `src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx:198-222`. `src/app/faq/page.tsx:141` and `src/app/tools/ev-to-sp/page.tsx:128` emit their own sets. The homepage defaults duplicate questions that also appear on `/faq` ("What is a VGC team report?", "How do I share a VGC team report?"). Duplicated `FAQPage` entities across URLs dilute which URL an engine binds each answer to.

Worse for the wedge: `DEFAULT_FAQ_ITEMS` contains no SP/Stat-Points question at all, so the homepage — our highest-authority URL — carries zero structured SP content.

### 2.6 🟠 A duplicate of the site is indexed on `*.vercel.app`
WebSearch returned `vgc-team-report.vercel.app` as a distinct result with its own description. There is **no `middleware.ts`** anywhere in the repo (checked repo root and `src/`), and **no `VERCEL_ENV`-conditional noindex** anywhere in `src/` or `next.config.ts` — the only `VERCEL_*` hits are unrelated. `public/robots.txt` is a static file with an absolute `Sitemap:` line, served identically from every deployment host. Given the CLAUDE.md deploy model (every push builds a preview; production only on Promote), every preview URL is crawlable and self-declares `Allow: /`.

Effect: authority splits across two hostnames, and an assistant may cite the vercel.app copy — which will silently drift from production. The canonical tags in `src/app/layout.tsx:82-84` and per-page `alternates.canonical` point at the apex domain, which mitigates but does not eliminate this for AI crawlers that weight the fetched host.

### 2.7 🟡 No machine-readable conversion endpoint
`src/app/api/champions/meta/route.ts` is the only Champions API route, and `robots.txt` `Disallow: /api/` (correctly) blocks the whole namespace. There is no citable, stable JSON representation of the conversion table anywhere — the natural home would be a static `public/` asset, which sidesteps the `/api/` disallow entirely.

### 2.8 🟢 What is already right (do not regress)
- `/tools/ev-to-sp` is `force-static`, has a tight canonical, real keyword targeting, 7 FAQ entries, two full conversion tables, and `<caption class="sr-only">` on both — and the tables are **generated by the same functions the app uses** (`evToChampionsSp` / `championsSpToEv`), so they cannot drift from product behaviour. That last property is rare and is a genuine citation asset.
- The page already answers the exact long-tail questions assistants get asked verbatim: *"What is 252 EVs in SP?"*, *"What is 4 EVs in SP?"*, *"Why does the EV to SP math not add up?"*
- FAQ headings carry slugified `id`s for deep-link anchors (`slugify` at `src/app/faq/page.tsx:29-37`) — explicitly built for AI citation anchors per `src/app/changelog/data.ts`.
- `/compare` and `/embed` correctly excluded from the sitemap / noindexed; `/s/[id]` noindexes collaborator-key URLs.

## 3. The SP wedge strategy

**The thesis.** We will not out-authority Pikalytics on "best team builder" or Bulbapedia on "what is a stat point." We *can* own the narrow, high-intent, mechanically-precise question: **how EVs convert to SP, and what any given spread costs.** Three conditions make this winnable right now:

1. **The fact is contested.** Live AI answers hedge between "≈8 EVs per SP" and the correct piecewise rule. Whoever states the rule unambiguously, with a derivation and a complete table, becomes the disambiguating citation.
2. **Nobody owns the *reverse* direction.** Every competitor converts EV→SP. Our `championsSpToEv` produces the full 1→32 ladder with the per-step EV cost — the "what did this SP spread cost me" question that no captured competitor answers.
3. **We have executable ground truth.** Our tables are function-generated and unit-tested. Competitors hand-type theirs, which is why "≈8 EVs" propagates. We can make correctness-under-drift an explicit, stated property of the page.

**The three assertions to own**, repeated verbatim and identically across page copy, FAQ answers, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and JSON-LD:

- `SP = ceil(EVs ÷ 8)`, floored at 1 for any non-zero EV value, capped at 32 from 248 EVs up.
- The first SP costs 4 EVs; every SP after it costs 8. (4→1, 12→2, 20→3, … 248→32.)
- 66 SP total per Pokémon, 32 SP hard cap per stat. 252/252/4 converts to 32/32/1 = 65 SP, leaving 1 spare.

**Consistency is the mechanism.** Retrieval engines reward a source that says the same thing in HTML, in structured data, and in the LLM-addressed text files. Right now `llms-full.txt` actively breaks that consistency (§2.1). Fixing that one file is worth more than any new content.

**Two claims nobody else is making**, both true of our implementation and both highly quotable:
- *"248 EVs and 252 EVs cost exactly the same in Champions"* — already on the page; it kills the classic bulk-optimisation habit and is a surprising, memorable fact.
- *"A 252 HP / 4 Def paste converts to 32 HP / 1 Def and leaves 33 SP unspent"* — from `stat-calculator.ts:193-200`. Naïve converters pad the spread to 66 and invent investment the player never bought. We deliberately do not (`stat-calculator.ts:226-231`). **"Why most EV→SP converters give you a spread you didn't build"** is a defensible, differentiating claim and the single best piece of content we could write.
