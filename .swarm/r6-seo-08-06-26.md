# R6 SEO Audit — 2026-06-08

Static read-only audit of `https://pokemonvgcteamreport.com` (Next.js 16 app at `/home/user/VGC-Team-Report`). Focus: quick wins in <100 LOC.

---

## Audit results by category

### 1. Sitemap (`src/app/sitemap.ts`)

What we have ✅
- Static homepage, `/explore`, `/champions`, `/faq`, `/tournaments`, `/compare`, `/changelog`, `/feedback`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.
- All Reg M-A megas with sprites at `/champions/:slug`.
- Up to 5000 public share pages `/s/:id` with `updated_at`.
- All distinct public creator pages `/creator/:name`.
- Sensible priorities and `changeFrequency`.

What's missing ❌
- **`/compare` is listed twice** (lines 16 and 18) with different priorities (0.6 vs 0.5). Duplicate entry, Google will dedupe but it's sloppy and confusing.
- No `lastModified` for `/champions/:slug`, `/creator/:name` pages — they fall back to "(no value)" so Google can't prioritize crawl.
- Sitemap silently swallows DB errors → if Neon is down at build, you ship a stripped sitemap with no shares/creators. No alerting.
- Long-tail filtered explore pages like `/explore?species=Incineroar` or `/explore?regulation=Reg+M-A` not present (these are high-intent queries).

### 2. Robots (`public/robots.txt`)

What we have ✅
- `Allow: /`, `Disallow: /api/`.
- Explicit allows for Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot.
- Sitemap URL declared.

What's missing ❌
- **Missing modern AI/SERP crawlers:** `Google-Extended` (Gemini training opt-in), `Applebot-Extended`, `Bytespider` (TikTok/Doubao), `CCBot` (Common Crawl), `Amazonbot`, `meta-externalagent`/`facebookexternalhit`, `DuckAssistBot`, `YouBot`, `cohere-ai`, `Diffbot`. The site is already AI-friendly (llms.txt, llms-full.txt) — these should be explicit.
- `/dashboard`, `/notifications`, `/api/team-graphic`, `/embed/` are not disallowed. They use `robots: { index:false, follow:false }` per-page so are technically fine, but adding `Disallow: /dashboard/`, `Disallow: /notifications` saves crawl budget.
- No `Crawl-delay` for noisy bots.

### 3. Per-page metadata

What we have ✅
- Most routes have `title`, `description`, `openGraph`, `twitter`, `alternates.canonical`.
- Champions detail and share routes produce dynamic metadata with placement/tournament context.
- `/dashboard/*` and `/notifications` correctly noindex.

What's missing ❌
- **`/privacy` and `/terms` are missing description, openGraph, twitter.** Privacy has only `title: "Privacy Policy"` + canonical. Terms only `title: "Terms of Service"` + canonical. No social embed at all, generic titles.
- **`/changelog` description (84 chars) and openGraph title don't follow the `%s | VGC Team Report` template** (it uses `—`); inconsistent with other routes.
- **`/feedback` missing keywords** and has a too-short description (75 chars).
- **`/dashboard/profile` page has no `export const metadata`** — relies on template default, so social embed is the generic site card; minor.
- Several titles overshoot 60 chars (Google SERP truncation ~580px):
  - `/explore`: **64 chars** ("Best VGC Teams 2026 — Explore Top Team Reports | VGC Team Report")
  - `/champions`: **65 chars** ("Pokemon Champions Format | Mega Evolution Teams — VGC Team Report")
- Several descriptions are too long (>160 chars, truncation risk in SERPs and Twitter cards):
  - Root layout description: **202 chars**.
  - `/explore`: **194 chars**.
  - `/tournaments`: **175 chars**.
- Several too short and weak:
  - `/changelog` (84), `/feedback` (75), `/privacy` (none), `/terms` (none), `/compare` (104, plus the page has `robots: { index:false }` so it's intentional).
- `/notifications` and `/dashboard` are correctly noindex but emit `openGraph` defaults pulled from layout — fine.

### 4. JSON-LD (`src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx`)

What we have ✅
- `WebSite` + `Organization` + `WebApplication/SoftwareApplication` in root layout.
- `BreadcrumbList` on champions, champions/[mega], faq, creator, changelog, tournaments.
- `ItemList` on /champions.
- `FAQPage` on /, /faq, and each /champions/[mega].
- `HowTo` on / and /faq.
- `SportsEvent` on /tournaments (2 upcoming).
- `CreativeWork` on /s/[id] with author and contributors.
- `ProfilePage` on /creator/[name].
- `CollectionPage` on /explore with nested breadcrumb.

What's missing ❌
- **No `SearchAction` keyword bound to a real searchbox on Explore.** The WebSite SearchAction in layout points to `/explore?q={search_term_string}` — verify that `q` is actually the param name used on /explore (it is, but worth confirming long-term).
- **No `ImageObject` / `primaryImageOfPage`** on champions/[mega]; we have an opengraph-image generator but it's not wired into JSON-LD as `image`.
- **No `Article` schema for changelog entries.** Each release note could be `BlogPosting` or `Article` for date-pinned indexing.
- **No `Product` / `SoftwareApplication` `AggregateRating`** anywhere — but you'd need real reviews.
- **No `VideoObject`** anywhere — Manraj's YouTube/X content could be cited.
- **No `Person` (sameAs) for verified creators.** Already have a `Person` minimal block in /creator/[name], but `sameAs: [twitter, youtube, discord]` would link the entity graph.
- **No `BreadcrumbList` on /s/[id]** — share pages currently get only `CreativeWork`. Adding Home → Explore → Team would create breadcrumb SERP display.
- **`OrganizationJsonLd` `sameAs` only lists GitHub.** Should include `x.com/Manny64Official`, the YouTube/Discord/etc. for entity disambiguation in Google's Knowledge Graph.

### 5. Heading hierarchy

What we have ✅
- Each landing route has exactly one `<h1>`:
  - `/` → PasteInput.tsx h1 (line 283)
  - `/explore` → ExploreHero.tsx h1
  - `/champions` → ChampionsContent.tsx h1
  - `/champions/[mega]` → MegaLandingContent.tsx h1
  - `/faq` → faq/page.tsx h1
  - `/tournaments` → TournamentsContent.tsx h1
  - `/compare` → CompareContent.tsx h1
  - `/creator/[name]` → CreatorProfile.tsx h1
  - `/not-found` → h1

What's missing ❌
- **`/changelog`** — no h1 found in ChangelogContent (only h2/h3 for entries). The page metadata has a title but the body lacks the top-level h1 → screen readers and Google see this as headless content.
- **`/dashboard/profile`** — h1 only shown when signed-in (line 245). Signed-out branch (line 229) has h1 "Sign in to edit your profile" — OK.
- **`/feedback` and `/notifications`** — depend on Content components; need to spot-check. Notifications uses `useTranslation` but the empty/signed-in body should have an h1.
- **Champions index h1** wraps an `<h1>` with `<br>` plus span — Google reads as "Pokemon Champions VGC Team Reports" which is fine, but consider single string.
- **TournamentsContent** uses `<h2>` for each card (line 105) — they're inside `<TournamentCard>` so render after the page h1 at line 268. DOM order is correct, but the cards are visible above the fold in some layouts — make sure h2 sections logically nest. Minor.

### 6. Internal linking

What we have ✅
- `PageFooter` renders Home / Explore / FAQ / Changelog / Privacy / Terms + Cookie Settings on most pages.
- `PersistentNavbar` renders sitewide except `/s/` and `/embed/`.
- Champion → explore links: `/champions/[mega]` has "View all →" → `/explore?species=…&regulation=Reg+M-A` (good).
- "Related Megas" grid on /champions/[mega] (8 cross-links).
- FAQ → home + explore CTAs.

What's missing ❌
- **`/s/[id]` (share pages) have NO footer.** The `redirect.tsx` strips chrome to mount the client SPA; there's no SEO-only crawlable footer. This means every public share page is an orphan from a link-graph perspective beyond the JSON-LD `isPartOf`. Adding a thin `<footer>` (visually hidden if needed) with links to Home / Explore / Champions / Tournaments would massively distribute internal PageRank.
- **No "Browse by Pokemon" link** from /champions → /explore?species=X (only "View all" on detail pages).
- **No `/tournaments` link in footer.** It's in the navbar but if Google walks the footer, it misses tournaments.
- **No links to `/champions` from `/explore`** (only banner from /).
- **`/faq` mid-content links** all go to `/` or `/explore` — could deep-link to `/champions/mega-kangaskhan`, `/tournaments`, etc., to spread anchor text.
- **No `prev`/`next` rel pagination** on champions/explore listing pages.

### 7. Image alt tags

What we have ✅
- Only two raw `<img>` tags in source: both have `alt` (s/[id]/opengraph-image at line 165, api/team-graphic at line 472).
- Embed page sprites (`embed/[id]/page.tsx`) have alt.
- The project does NOT use `next/image` anywhere — all images use raw `<img>` (sprite slugs, profile pic, OG generators). This is OK if alt coverage is comprehensive.

What's missing ❌
- **No `next/image` adoption anywhere** — every Pokémon sprite is a raw `<img>` from `play.pokemonshowdown.com`. This means:
  - No automatic responsive `srcset` → larger LCP on mobile.
  - No image optimization or modern-format conversion.
  - No automatic `loading="lazy"` (must add manually — most sprite renders don't have it).
- Cypress smoke-check or lint rule should enforce `alt` on all new `<img>`.

### 8. Title/description lengths (see flags above)

Summary:
- **Over-length titles:** `/explore` (64), `/champions` (65).
- **Over-length descriptions:** root layout (202), `/explore` (194), `/tournaments` (175).
- **Under-length descriptions:** `/changelog` (84), `/feedback` (75), `/privacy` (none), `/terms` (none).
- All mega-detail titles fit (≤57 chars).

## Top 10 quick wins

Ranked by SEO leverage × ease. All <100 LOC each, no risky file conflicts.

### 1. Add description + OpenGraph + Twitter to /privacy and /terms
- **File:** `src/app/privacy/page.tsx`, `src/app/terms/page.tsx`
- **Change:** Extend `metadata` block to include `description`, `openGraph`, `twitter`, robots index. ~12 LOC each = 24 LOC.
- **Impact:** Legal pages currently embed as a generic site card in Discord/X. Better social embed + clearer GDPR/CCPA signal to crawlers. Removes "thin metadata" flag in Lighthouse.

### 2. Tighten over-length titles + descriptions in root layout, /explore, /tournaments
- **Files:** `src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/app/explore/page.tsx`, `src/app/tournaments/page.tsx`
- **Change:**
  - Layout desc → trim 202 to ≤160 (cut the trailing "Supports Pokemon Champions 2026, Mega Evolution, and all VGC formats." into something tighter).
  - `/explore` title → drop " | VGC Team Report" suffix to drop from 64→48 (template adds nothing here; `template: "%s | VGC Team Report"` collides with the explicit suffix → double-branding).
  - `/explore` desc → cut 194 to ~155.
  - `/champions` title → drop "— VGC Team Report" to drop 65→50.
  - `/tournaments` desc → cut 175 to ~155.
  - ~20 LOC total.
- **Impact:** Removes SERP truncation. The double "| VGC Team Report" suffix issue (template + explicit) appears on /explore, /champions, /faq, /tournaments — currently rendering as ".. | VGC Team Report | VGC Team Report" risk. Verify.

### 3. Remove duplicate /compare entry from sitemap; add `lastModified` everywhere
- **File:** `src/app/sitemap.ts`
- **Change:** Delete line 18 (`{ url: \`${BASE}/compare\`, priority: 0.5 }`), add `lastModified: now` to mega + creator entries. ~6 LOC.
- **Impact:** Cleaner sitemap, Google trusts crawl freshness signals, /compare prioritized correctly.

### 4. Add Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, meta-externalagent, etc. to robots.txt
- **File:** `public/robots.txt`
- **Change:** ~30 LOC block of `User-agent: X / Allow: /`. Add `Disallow: /dashboard/` and `Disallow: /notifications` under `User-agent: *`.
- **Impact:** Eligibility for Gemini, Apple Intelligence, Common Crawl-fed training/citations. Already winning AI citations; this expands coverage. Saves crawl budget on auth-required routes.

### 5. Add a crawlable, lightweight `<footer>` to /s/[id] share pages
- **File:** `src/app/s/[id]/redirect.tsx` (NOT excluded; check whether SlideNavControls etc. are involved). If conflict-risky, instead add it server-side in `src/app/s/[id]/page.tsx` (allowed).
- **Change:** Append a simple `<footer>` with links to `/`, `/explore`, `/champions`, `/tournaments`, `/faq`. Wrap in `<noscript>` or always-render for crawlers. ~25 LOC.
- **Impact:** Currently the highest-traffic page type (`/s/:id`) has zero outbound links besides JSON-LD. Adds internal PageRank distribution across thousands of share pages — biggest leverage move on the list.

### 6. Add `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD to /s/[id]
- **File:** `src/app/s/[id]/page.tsx`
- **Change:** ~10 LOC. Items: Home → Explore → (tournament name or species) → current share.
- **Impact:** Google shows breadcrumbs in SERP for individual reports, improving CTR.

### 7. Expand `OrganizationJsonLd.sameAs`
- **File:** `src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx`
- **Change:** Add `https://x.com/Manny64Official` (already in footer) + any YouTube/Discord URLs to `sameAs`. ~5 LOC.
- **Impact:** Helps Google build the entity graph and surface "VGC Team Report" as a recognized brand in knowledge panels.

### 8. Add h1 to /changelog and missing pages
- **File:** `src/app/changelog/ChangelogContent.tsx`
- **Change:** Add a single `<h1>Changelog</h1>` (or "VGC Team Report Changelog") at the top of the content. ~3 LOC.
- **Impact:** Removes "no h1" Lighthouse penalty; clearer doc structure for crawlers and assistive tech.

### 9. Add long-tail static landing routes inside sitemap: /explore filter combinations
- **File:** `src/app/sitemap.ts`
- **Change:** Append ~10 hard-coded explore filter URLs to sitemap (no new routes — `/explore?species=Incineroar`, `?regulation=Reg+M-A`, `?eventType=Worlds`, etc.). ~15 LOC.
- **Impact:** Surfaces filtered explore pages that already render server-side. Targets long-tail queries like "Incineroar VGC", "Regulation H teams". Free clicks.

### 10. Add `loading="lazy"` + width/height to all raw sprite `<img>` in MegaLandingContent and embed
- **Files:** `src/components/explore/ReportCard.tsx`, `src/app/champions/[pokemon]/MegaLandingContent.tsx`, `src/app/embed/[id]/page.tsx`
- **Change:** Add `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` to all sprite `<img>` not in the hero. ~15 LOC across files.
- **Impact:** Faster LCP on listing pages → ranking signal for Core Web Vitals. Embeds become friendlier to host pages.

## Long-tail keyword opportunities

High-intent VGC queries the codebase isn't directly targeting yet. Each could be served by either a metadata tweak on an existing route, or a new keyword-targeted block inside an existing landing.

### Format/regulation queries
- "Regulation H VGC teams" — `/explore?regulation=Reg+H` exists but isn't in sitemap or linked from FAQ/champions. Add a brief "By regulation" linkset on /explore or FAQ.
- "Regulation I teams 2026" — same pattern.
- "Worlds 2025 winning team" — `/explore?eventType=Worlds` exists; add a static landing or a metadata-rich sitemap entry.
- "VGC Champions Regulation MA" / "Reg M-A" — covered by /champions but title could front-load the slug term ("Pokemon Champions Reg M-A — Mega Evolution Teams").

### Pokemon-specific queries (already partially covered by /champions/[mega])
- "{Mega} EV spread" / "{Mega} EVs" — title currently says "SP Spreads" but EV is the more searched term. Add "EV Spreads" to title or H1 as a synonym (the FAQ schema text already covers it).
- "{Mega} damage calc" — every /champions/[mega] should mention damage calc capability above the fold.
- "Calyrex-Ice VGC team", "Miraidon team report", "Incineroar VGC set" — non-mega Pokémon don't have landing pages. Either create `/explore?species=Calyrex-Ice` landing entries (via sitemap) or build a `/pokemon/[name]` route stub.

### Tournament/event queries
- "VGC Indianapolis Regionals 2026 results" — currently a single SportsEvent JSON-LD block on /tournaments. Could be its own slug `/tournaments/indianapolis-2026`.
- "NAIC 2026 VGC teams", "EUIC 2026 results", "Worlds 2026 SF" — each deserves a per-event title hook.
- "VGC Worlds qualified players" — could become a leaderboard / hub page.

### Tool/format queries (AEO angle)
- "How to share a Pokemon team online" — covered by HowToSchema; the FAQ entry exists but no dedicated `/share-vgc-team` slug.
- "Pokepaste alternative" — listed in keywords on /faq; could rank a comparison subsection.
- "Speed tier calculator VGC" — not currently in titles. Add to /explore or a /tools page.
- "OTS sheet generator" / "Open team sheet VGC" — the FAQ explains it but no dedicated landing. Could go in `/explore?hasOTS=1` or a `/tools/ots-generator` route.
- "VGC damage calc with team context" — Pikalytics ranks here; we have inline calc support but don't market the keyword.

### Coach / creator queries
- "{CreatorName} team report" — `/creator/[name]` covers this but creator pages aren't linked from explore cards prominently. Already in sitemap.
- "Wolfey team report" / "Aaron Zheng team" / popular VGC creator names → low-risk metadata addition to creator pages with `keywords` listing tournament finishes.

### Comparison queries
- "VGC Team Report vs Pokepaste" — only inside FAQ body. Should be a meta `keywords` addition to /faq plus a heading anchor (#vgc-team-report-vs-pokepaste).
- "VGC.tools alternative" — same.

### Quick wins for long-tail
A single sitemap append of ~15 filtered `/explore` URLs (regulation × eventType × top 5 species), plus adding `keywords` to /privacy and /terms (e.g. "VGC Team Report GDPR", "Pokemon team report privacy"), unlocks several easy clicks with zero new code.
