### Crawler gating in `src/proxy.ts` — still clean

`www` is still never redirected to the apex (`proxy.ts:66`). Both hosts serve 200; self-canonicals point at the apex so Google will consolidate. One-line fix, low value.

## 5. Internal linking, crawl depth, heading hierarchy

### The navbar: F2 is now diagnosed, and the fix is one file

October reported the navbar marker in 0 of 95 prerendered files. Tonight it is in **1 of 98** — `tools/ev-to-sp.html`, which renders **11 crawlable nav links** (`/`, `/explore`, `/champions`, `/compare`, `/changelog`, `/feedback`, `/dashboard`, `/faq`, `/tools/ev-to-sp`, `/support`, `/privacy`, `/terms`).

That one difference is the diagnosis. `/tools/ev-to-sp` is the only page with `export const dynamic = "force-static"`, which makes `useSearchParams()` resolve statically instead of bailing the enclosing Suspense boundary to its `fallback={null}`. So:

- `PageNavbar` itself is **fully SSR-safe** — including Clerk's `useAuth()` and `LanguageSelector`. Proven by that file.
- The only thing suppressing it sitewide is `PersistentNavbar`'s top-level `useSearchParams()` (`PersistentNavbar.tsx:36`), used solely to compute `isShareContext` from `?s=`.
- `usePathname()` does **not** bail, and `PersistentNavbar` already runs a `useEffect` that reads `window.location.hash`.

**So F2 collapses to: move the `?s=` read out of `useSearchParams` and into that existing effect.** One file, ~10 lines, and the navbar prerenders on all 98 pages. Honest caveat: on a `/?s=<id>` URL the navbar would render for one frame before the effect hides it. That is a visible-flash regression on share links, traded against a dead sitewide link graph — worth gating behind a `mounted` check or accepting deliberately, but it must be a conscious call, not a surprise.

- `/champions` → 81 mega pages works properly, and the rotating `relatedMegas` window (offset by the current page's index rather than a fixed `.slice(0,8)`) remains a genuinely good piece of internal-linking engineering.
