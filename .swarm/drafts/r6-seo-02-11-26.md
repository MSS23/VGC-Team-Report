### 6b. Body copy — the "Regulation M-A" line in the page body (~line 345)

Current: `… for Pokémon Champions Regulation M-A …`
Draft:   `… for Pokémon Champions Regulation M-C — and M-A and M-B, which M-C is a superset of …`

Optional explainer paragraph for the same page (uses only facts already in
`src/lib/analysis/stat-calculator.ts` and `src/lib/validation/champions-legality.ts` —
do not publish any claim this repo's own tests do not lock):

> **How Stat Points work.** Pokémon Champions replaces EVs with Stat Points: 66 per Pokémon,
> maximum 32 in any single stat. The conversion is not linear — the first Stat Point is worth
> 4 EVs and every point after it is worth 8, so a 252-EV investment is 32 SP, and a 4-EV
> "leftover" is 1 SP. This converter applies the exact same maths the report builder uses, so a
> spread that validates here will validate in a legality check.

> ⚠️ **Verify the 4-then-8 conversion against the official Champions rules before publishing
> this paragraph.** This repo implements first-SP = 4 EVs / subsequent = 8 EVs and locks it with
> a test, but at least one third-party calculator models 1 SP = +1 to the final stat. The prior
> run could not adjudicate it and neither could this one (no network). Do not publish an
> authoritative-sounding explainer on a disputed mechanic until a human checks the rules doc.

## Quick win #1 — if the mega-page FAQ is rendered rather than deleted

The 6 questions in `src/app/champions/[pokemon]/page.tsx:244-277` are all derived from
first-party data and are safe to render as-is. Suggested visible section for
`MegaLandingContent.tsx`, replacing the comment block at `:205-211`, placed AFTER the

```tsx
{faqs.length > 0 && (
  <section className="max-w-5xl mx-auto px-4 py-8">
    <h2 className="text-xl font-extrabold text-text-primary mb-4">
      {mega.displayName} — Common Questions
    </h2>
    <dl className="space-y-3">
      {faqs.map((f) => (
        <div key={f.q} className="rounded-xl border border-border bg-surface p-4">
          <dt className="text-sm font-bold text-text-primary">{f.q}</dt>
          <dd className="text-sm text-text-secondary mt-1 leading-relaxed">{f.a}</dd>
        </div>
      ))}

```
This requires passing `faqs` back through as a prop (the type comment at
`MegaLandingContent.tsx:81` records that it was removed). A `<dl>` keeps the Q&A pairing
semantic, which also satisfies the FAQPage policy requirement that the content be visible.

**If instead the schema is deleted:** remove the whole `<JsonLd data={{ "@type": "FAQPage" … }}>`
block at `page.tsx:274-325` and the now-unused `faqItems` array at `:244-277`, and update the
stale comment at `:238-239` ("Google surfaces these as rich snippets for long-tail queries" —
