## Notes on Outreach Strategy

**Timing:** Best time to reach creators is 1-3 days after a major Regional or International, when they're in "post-tournament sharing mode" and the friction of the current workflow is most salient.

**What to lead with:**
- Acknowledge their specific workflow (don't send a generic pitch)
- Reference a specific team or tournament result they've done recently
- Keep the ask small: "would love feedback" not "please promote this"

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't lead with monetization angle to Aaron Zheng (he's open-sharing oriented)
- Don't imply the site competes with Patreon for Wolfey/THATSAplusONE — frame it as complementary
- Don't send to multiple creators simultaneously in a way that looks mass-outreach

**Creator priority order based on research:**
1. @VGCPastes — highest leverage, community infrastructure role, low friction ask
2. @WolfeyGlick — highest visibility, Patreon-complementary angle is strong
3. @CybertronVGC — open sharing model, good fit for creator profile feature
4. @JamesWBaek — YouTube-focused, team report as video supplement angle
5. @PhillipWingett (THATSAplusONE) — high volume, Patreon teaser angle
