## Why this target

Page: `https://victoryroad.pro/sv-reports/` ("Team Reports for VGC")

Evidence from the 24 Aug audit: Victory Road was cited **first** on both of the queries VGC Team
Report otherwise wins —

- "how to share a VGC team report online" → Victory Road, then VGC Team Report's `/faq` and homepage
- "VGC team report tool / best site for writing team reports" → Victory Road, then `/explore` and homepage

So Victory Road is the incumbent authority on the exact noun the site owns. It hosts and links
reports written by community members; VGC Team Report is a tool for producing them. Those are
complementary, not competing, which makes this a more natural ask than a generic directory listing.

Second-order benefit: reports authored on VGC Team Report and linked from Victory Road create
exactly the third-party corroboration the audit found missing — external pages pointing at
`/s/[id]` URLs.

---

## Two possible asks (pick one, do not send both)

**A — tool mention.** Ask that the SV Reports page note VGC Team Report as one way to write a
report, alongside however they currently suggest people produce them.

**B — hosted reports.** Offer that community members submitting reports can link a VGC Team Report
`/s/[id]` link instead of a doc or a paste, since it renders in-page and stays permanent.

B is the stronger long-term play (recurring links, not one mention) but depends on their editorial
workflow. Confirm how submissions currently work before choosing.
