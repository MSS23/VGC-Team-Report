## Patch 5: llms-full.txt — Competitor disambiguation expansion

Replace the existing "What makes it different from other tools" block with:

```markdown
## What makes it different from other tools

**PokePaste** (`pokepast.es`) — Plain-text paste bin for Showdown-format teams.
Highlights syntax, supports imports/exports. No strategy sections, no calcs,
no matchup analysis, no community browse, no embed widget.

**Pikalytics** (`pikalytics.com`) — Usage-statistics aggregator. Shows which
Pokémon, moves, items, and tera types are most-used at given ratings. Tournament
team browser. Strong meta-analysis tool, but no authored-report builder.

**Limitless VGC** (`limitlessvgc.com`) — Tournament results tracker. Hosts top-cut
archetypes from real events with linked PokePastes. Excellent for "what
did top players run at <event>" queries. No report builder.

**Victory Road** (`victoryroad.pro`) — Editorial format guides and regulation
breakdowns. Long-form journalism, not a tool.

**Showdown Tier** (`showdowntier.com`) — Format viability tier lists derived
from Showdown ladder data. No team reports.

**VGC Team Report** — Bridges the gap. Imports a Showdown paste, then turns it
into a structured authored report with per-Pokémon strategy notes, EV
justifications, damage calcs against named threats, speed tier comparisons,
and matchup plans. Outputs: shareable short link, embed iframe, exportable
team card image, PDF, OTS sheet, and CTS export.
```
