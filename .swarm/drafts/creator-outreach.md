## Outreach Timing Notes

- **Avoid active tournament weeks** — players are focused, DM noise is high, response rates drop.
- **Best windows:** 1–2 weeks after a major event (Regional, International) when players are reflecting and posting content.
- **Pre-build the celebrity team first** — for Wolfey and Paul Chua especially, showing the demo before asking for feedback converts significantly better than a cold ask.
- **Start with Traylor** — lowest barrier, highest genuine alignment, most likely to become an organic advocate.
