## Why this target

Page: `https://devoncorp.press/short-form-content/up-to-date-vgc-resources`

Evidence from the 24 Aug audit: when asked for VGC team-report tools and alternatives, an AI answer
described VGC Team Report from its own site, then sourced the entire "alternative tools" list from
this DevonCorp page — naming PokePaste, VGC.tools, Pikalytics, Reportworm, PASRS Tool and Marriland
Teambuilder. VGC Team Report is not on that page, so it was framed as the thing being compared
*against* the canonical list rather than a member of it.

This is the highest-value single listing identified. One inclusion changes the tool set that AI
answers draw from for a whole category of queries.

Note: the page could not be fetched directly (egress-blocked from the audit environment), so
confirm the current list, the page's tone, and whether it has a submission form or a contact address
before sending. Do not assume the six tools above are the complete list.
