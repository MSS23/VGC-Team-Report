## 1. Positioning one-liners (internal use — pick one, then pressure-test)

Framed on the one axis the teardown supports: we are the *share artifact*, and no
competitor hosts the user's own team as a readable, explained object.

- "Paste your team, get a page worth sending."
- "A team paste says what you brought. A team report says why."
- "The link you send after you've built the team."
- "Showdown paste in. Something a teammate can actually read out."

Avoid: anything of the form "better than Poképaste". Poképaste is a free,
open-source, community-load-bearing project and is wired into Showdown itself.
Positioning against it reads as hostile and invites the correct reply that it does
its one job perfectly.

**Preferred frame: complement, not replacement.** "Keep using Poképaste to move the
team into Showdown; use a report when you want to explain it." This is also simply
true — Showdown imports Poképaste links natively, and we should not pretend to
replace that loop.
