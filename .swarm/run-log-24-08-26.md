## Known gap shipped knowingly

`og:image` now points at `/api/team-graphic`, but `robots.txt` disallows `/api/` and **Twitterbot
honours robots.txt**, so X may decline to fetch it. Discord, Slack and Facebook ignore robots.txt
and unfurl correctly. The fix is a non-`/api/` alias route for the graphic — *not* a robots.txt
edit, which would reintroduce the VGC-272 bug. Queued.

Also unresolved: `/api/team-graphic` keeps `apiGuard` rate limiting at 10/min per IP, which a
crawler pool could trip. `s-maxage=86400` means the edge absorbs nearly all unfurl traffic, so this
is unlikely but not impossible.
