# Competitor Analysis: VGCpastes, Limitless VGC, Trainer Hill

**Date:** 2026-05-27

---

## 1. VGCpastes (Google Sheets + pokepast.es ecosystem)

**Features:** Crowd-sourced Google Sheets repository of tournament teams, organized by regulation (Reg I has 63+ teams, Reg H had 1,150+). Links out to pokepast.es for individual team views. Active Twitter/Discord community for submissions.

**Share/Export UX:** Teams are plain-text pokepaste links. No sprites, no OG image embeds, no visual preview. Users copy a pokepast.es URL and share it manually. No in-app export to rental code, image, or PDF.

**Monetization:** None. Fully volunteer-run, no ads, no Patreon.

**Strengths over a team report builder:** Massive volume of real tournament teams; zero friction to browse; familiar pokepaste format every VGC player already knows.

**Known issues:** Google Sheets does not scale well for discovery or search; no visual display; no metadata (writeups, matchup notes, EV explanations); relies entirely on community submissions with no quality filter.

**Recent updates (2025-26):** Added Regulation I and Champions-era teams. Expanded to 2,600+ pastes via sister aggregator VGenC.

---

## 2. Limitless VGC (limitlessvgc.com)

**Features:** Tournament database with full standings, pairings, usage stats, win rates, and conversion rates. Team lists for top-placing players at every major event. Player profiles with historical performance. Metagame overviews updated per round showing archetype records. Standings site (standings.limitlessvgc.com) added in 2025 for deep per-tournament data. Also runs online tournaments via play.limitlesstcg.com.

**Share/Export UX:** Teams displayed in pokepaste format within tournament context. No dedicated share button or image export; users link directly to the tournament page.

**Monetization:** Ad-supported free tier. Patreon for ad-free experience.

**Strengths over a team report builder:** Authoritative tournament data source; usage stats and win-rate analytics that no team builder offers; player ranking system; real competitive context around every team.

**Known issues:** No user-generated team reports or writeups; teams only appear if they top-cut an official event; no way for casual players to publish their own teams; UX is data-dense but not beginner-friendly.

**Recent updates (2025-26):** Launched standings.limitlessvgc.com with full pairings and team lists. Expanded to Pokemon Champions format. 542+ tracked players as of May 2026.

---

## 3. Trainer Hill (trainerhill.com)

**Features:** TCG and TCG Pocket analytics hub -- **not VGC**. Meta trends, decklist analysis, matchup stats, tier lists, deck comparisons. "Battle Journal+" for match tracking. Open-source on GitHub.

**Share/Export UX:** Decklist search with filters and win rates. No VGC team sharing.

**Monetization:** Patreon (members vote on new game support). Battle Journal+ at $3/month after 7-day free trial.

**Strengths:** Strong analytics tooling and paid conversion model worth studying. Patreon community voting is a smart engagement tactic.

**Known issues:** TCG-only scope means it is not a direct VGC competitor. Community is smaller than Limitless.

**Recent updates (2025-26):** Added Pokemon TCG Pocket support. Battle Journal+ launched as premium product.

---

## Key Takeaways for VGC Team Report

| Gap in market | Who fails here | Our opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Visual, embeddable team display | VGCpastes (plain text), Limitless (data tables) | Rich OG images, Discord/Twitter embeds |
| User-generated writeups & narratives | All three | Team *reports* with strategy notes, not just pastes |
| Casual player publishing | Limitless (top-cut only) | Let anyone publish, not just tournament winners |
| Searchable team discovery | VGCpastes (spreadsheet) | Filterable database with Pokemon/archetype search |
| Freemium analytics | Trainer Hill model ($3/mo) | Match tracking, personal winrate stats as premium |
