## 3. robots

**No `src/app/robots.ts`.** Static file at `/home/user/VGC-Team-Report/public/robots.txt`.

```
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/
+ explicit Allow blocks for Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot
Sitemap: https://pokemonvgcteamreport.com/sitemap.xml
```

**Correctness: good.** Sitemap directive present and correct. `/api/` disallowed. AI-crawler allowlist is a deliberate and sensible AI-SEO posture.

**Findings:**

- ⚠️ The per-bot `User-agent: Googlebot / Allow: /` groups **override** the wildcard group for those agents. Under the robots.txt spec the most specific matching group wins *entirely* — so **Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and OAI-SearchBot are NOT subject to `Disallow: /api/`.** Those six groups have no `Disallow` line at all. `Allow: /` after `User-agent: *  Allow: /` is redundant anyway. Either delete the six redundant groups or repeat `Disallow: /api/` inside each.
- No `Disallow` for `/dashboard`, `/notifications`, `/embed`. Acceptable — all are meta-noindex'd, and meta-noindex only works if the crawler is *allowed* to fetch the page. Leaving them crawlable is the correct choice.
- Static `robots.txt` cannot vary by environment. Vercel preview deployments (`*.vercel.app`) will serve `Allow: /` and a prod-domain `Sitemap:` line. Low risk (previews aren't linked) but a `src/app/robots.ts` returning `Disallow: /` when `VERCEL_ENV !== "production"` would be strictly better.
- Consider migrating to `src/app/robots.ts` for parity with `sitemap.ts`.
