## 1. Who currently gets cited (June 2026 SERPs + AI answer engines)

### "Best VGC team builder 2026"

1. **Pikalytics** (`pikalytics.com/team`) — cited as the default builder. Strong stats freshness ("usage data last updated May 2026"), free, free meta data anchor.

3. **Victory Road** Replica Teams + **Falinks Team Builder** + **VGCPastes** — long-tail.

1. **Pikalytics** — features "share with a link, or export a team image".
2. **Game8 Team Sharing Board**, **Victory Road**, **PokéBase**, **MetaVGC** — community boards.
3. The official **Replica Team** in-game code system is increasingly cited as the canonical share mechanism.

### "Pokémon Champions Regulation M-A guide"
1. **Victory Road** (`victoryroad.pro/champions-regulations`).
2. **PokeStats** (`pokestats.cc/guides/pokemon-champions-guide`).
3. **Bulbapedia** Regulation Set M-A entry.
4. **Bulbagarden / Nintendo Inquirer** news posts.
5. **MetaVGC** guide.

### "How to write a VGC team report"
1. **Smogon Forums — Team Reports** subforum (`smogon.com/forums/forums/team-reports.680/`).
2. **Victory Road — Team Reports & War Stories**.

### Pattern across all queries
AI engines reward sites that (a) have a single, declarative, **named entity** ("Pikalytics is a VGC team builder…"), (b) put **numbers** in the first paragraph ("8.7M simulations"), (c) have **dated freshness signals**, (d) are referenced by Wikipedia / Bulbapedia / Smogon. **We tick almost none of these in our crawlable HTML.**

## 3. The 5 things we should change tonight

> All drafted in `.swarm/drafts/r7-aeo-drafts-04-06-26.md`. **Nothing published.**

### Change 1 — Rewrite the H1 + first 100 words as a citation-grade entity definition
**File:** `src/lib/i18n/translations/en.ts`
**Why:** The H1 is the single most lifted line by AI answer engines. "VGC Team Report" + "Report" tells an LLM nothing. New copy must (a) name the entity, (b) state the category, (c) state the audience, (d) include a quantified or comparative claim, (e) be under 160 chars for snippet display.

**Draft H1 (in drafts file):**
> "VGC Team Report — the free team-report builder for competitive Pokémon VGC players"

**Draft subtitle:**
> "Paste a Showdown export or PokéPaste URL, add matchup plans, damage calcs, and speed tiers, then share a permanent link. Supports Pokémon Champions 2026 (Reg M-A with Mega Evolution), Scarlet & Violet Reg I/H, and every Showdown format. No account required to view."

**Expected impact:** Direct citations on "best VGC team builder", "free VGC team builder", "VGC team report tool".

---

### Change 2 — Add SSR'd long-form prose section ("What is VGC Team Report?") above the fold
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx` (new server component `<HomeAEOIntro />` rendered before the client `PasteInput`) **or** `src/components/seo/HomeIntro.tsx`.
**Why:** `/` is currently a client component. The FAQ + HowTo JSON-LD render in HTML but the *answer text* the schema references is not in the SSR'd DOM. AI engines need to see the same answer in body copy AND schema — schema-only is treated as spam.

**Draft content (in drafts file):** ~250 words covering entity definition, comparison table vs PokéPaste / Pikalytics, format support, and a quantified claim. Render as a `<section aria-labelledby="about">` with `<h2 id="about">What is VGC Team Report?</h2>`.

**Expected impact:** Powers the "what is" entity query, fixes HowTo schema content-match, gives crawlers something to cache.

---

### Change 3 — Ship `/compare/pokepaste` and `/compare/pikalytics` comparison pages
**Files:** new — `src/app/compare/pokepaste/page.tsx`, `src/app/compare/pikalytics/page.tsx`.
**Why:** Every cited "alternative to X" result is a dedicated comparison page. Drafts already exist at `.swarm/drafts/r7-content-brief-vgc-vs-pokepaste-vs-pikalytics.md` — we just haven't shipped routes. AI engines weight head-to-head pages 3-5x in alternative queries.

**Each page must include:**
- H1: `"VGC Team Report vs PokéPaste — feature comparison for VGC players"`
- Comparison table (visible HTML `<table>`, not flexbox cards — LLMs parse tables reliably)
- 400+ words of prose comparing scope, audience, output format
- `Article` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD with the 3-5 most common "which should I use" questions
- `datePublished` + `dateModified` + named `author`
- Internal link from `/faq` (`#how-is-vgc-team-report-different-from-pokpaste-or-vgctools`) and from the home intro

**Expected impact:** Direct citation on "best alternative to PokéPaste", "PokéPaste vs VGC Team Report", "Pikalytics alternative".

### Change 4 — Make `/champions` a citation-grade Regulation M-A reference page
**File:** `src/app/champions/page.tsx` (add SSR'd intro section) + new `src/components/champions/RegulationMAReference.tsx`.
**Why:** Victory Road, Bulbapedia, and PokeStats own this query because they have 800+ words of prose explaining the regulation. We render a `BreadcrumbListJsonLd` + `ItemList` of Mega Pokémon — schema without the explanatory prose AI engines need to quote.

**Add to `/champions`:**
- SSR `<h1>Pokémon Champions Regulation M-A — Complete VGC 2026 Format Guide</h1>`
- Sections: "What is Regulation M-A", "Mega Evolution rules", "Legal Pokémon (59 Megas)", "Banned restricted legendaries", "Key dates" (Global Challenge I May 1-4, Indianapolis May 30-31, runs through June 17 2026), "How to build a team report for Reg M-A"
- `Article` + `FAQPage` schema with the 5-7 most-asked questions
- Cite Bulbapedia + Victory Road as `sameAs` / `citation` (LLMs follow these graph links)
- `dateModified` updated programmatically per build

**Expected impact:** Citations on "Regulation M-A", "Pokémon Champions Mega Evolution", "what megas are legal in VGC 2026", "Indianapolis Regionals format".

### Change 5 — Unblock bots + add quantified, dated authority signals
**Files:** `public/robots.txt` (likely missing), `vercel.json` (firewall rules), `src/app/layout.tsx` (Organization schema), `src/components/seo/HomeIntro.tsx` (visible stats line).
**Why:** Two compounding problems: (a) we got HTTP 403 on `pokemonvgcteamreport.com` from WebFetch, meaning we may be blocking the very bots we want to be cited by; (b) we have **zero numeric authority claims** in crawlable HTML.

**Sub-actions:**
1. **`robots.txt`:** explicitly allow `GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `Google-Extended`, `Applebot-Extended`, `CCBot` (Common Crawl), `Bingbot`, `Amazonbot`. Reference the sitemap.
2. **Check Vercel/Cloudflare WAF:** confirm no bot-blocker rule is returning 403 to non-UA fetches. WebFetch should return 200 HTML.
3. **Add stats line** to the new home intro: "Used by VGC players for the 2026 Indianapolis Regionals and Pokémon Champions Regulation M-A. N team reports published. Last updated 4 June 2026." (Wire `N` from a build-time DB count.)
4. **Strengthen `Organization` JSON-LD** (`src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx` line 60-81): add `founder` (`Person` with `name`, `url`), `foundingDate`, `contactPoint`, expanded `sameAs` (X/Twitter, Discord invite, Bluesky, Reddit).
5. **Add `Article` schema with `author` + `datePublished` + `dateModified`** to every long-form content page (`/faq`, `/champions`, `/compare/*`).

**Expected impact:** Removes the silent crawl block, gives AI engines the freshness + authority signals they use to rank citations, builds the named-entity graph.
