## Notes

- `en.ts` already has 290+ keys and good coverage of core report UI. The gap is almost entirely **ShareModal** (817 lines, zero translation calls) and a handful of hardcoded labels in ExploreFilters static arrays (`CATEGORIES`, `SORT_OPTIONS`, `PLACEMENTS`).
- `src/app/page.tsx` `HOW_TO_STEPS` is SEO/structured-data content; lower priority than interactive UI strings but worth extracting for completeness.
- `aria-label` values throughout ShareModal (e.g. `"Close"`, `"Copy link"`, `"Copy embed code"`, `"Dismiss thank you message"`) are also hardcoded and should be extracted alongside the visible strings for screen-reader support in non-English locales.
