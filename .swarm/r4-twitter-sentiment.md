# R4: Twitter/X VGC Creator Sentiment — Team-Sharing Tools (Updated)
**Research Date:** 2026-05-27
**Scope:** Twitter/X sentiment from top VGC players and community on team-sharing tools, workflows, and pain points.

---

## 1. Tools Top Players Use to Share Teams

| Creator | Primary Sharing Method | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Wolfey (@WolfeyGlick) | Tweet + Pokepaste link, full spreads gated on Patreon | Rental codes for broad reach |
| Cybertron (@CybertronVGC) | Free YouTube/Twitter educational content; co-authored VGCguide.com (100K+ words) | Brand deals, no paywall |

**Community infrastructure:** VGCPastes (1,150+ Reg H teams), Victory Road (editorial reports + rental codes), Limitless VGC (tournament DB), Pikalytics (data-driven builder), VGCHelper (mobile builder + image export), VGC.tools, Falinks Team Builder, Champions Lab, Champions Builder.

---

## 2. Complaints About Current Sharing Workflows

1. **Pokepaste is plain text** — no sprites, no visuals, no narrative context. Twitter/X is image-first; paste links get minimal organic engagement.
2. **No self-serve report tool** — Victory Road requires editorial gatekeeping; Medium lacks VGC-specific formatting (no inline paste rendering, no damage calc embeds).
3. **Pokepaste UX issues** — users report inability to select URLs in the notes box without inspect element; missing Pokemon HOME renders for newer forms.
4. **Fragmented discovery** — teams scattered across VGCPastes, Victory Road, Limitless, Patreon, Medium, YouTube, Discord. No single searchable surface.
5. **No tiered visibility** — creators like Wolfey gate spreads on Patreon as a workaround; no tool natively supports public-shell + private-spread reports.
6. **No attribution system** — team credit disputes (Wolfey vs. Brady Smith, NAIC 2023) drove major community drama; no canonical attribution at the paste layer.

---

## 3. Mentions of VGC Team Report

- pokemonvgcteamreport.com appears in search results alongside competitors but has **no visible Twitter/X creator endorsements** from top players.
- No evidence of Wolfey, Cybertron, James Baek, Aaron Zheng, or other top creators mentioning the tool on Twitter.
- The site is indexed and discoverable via search, but lacks social proof from the competitive community.
- **Gap:** The tool exists and is functional but has zero creator-layer awareness. This is the primary growth bottleneck.

---

## 4. Features Creators Want

| Feature | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Visual team card (shareable image for Twitter) | VGCHelper added image export v1.2.0; high engagement on image tweets |
| Tiered visibility (public/private spreads) | Wolfey Patreon gating behavior |
| Structured report editor (paste + narrative + calcs) | Aaron Traylor uses Medium as workaround |
| Replica/rental code embedding | Pokemon Champions launch (2026) — players share codes within hours of wins |
| Attribution/original-creator field | Community demand after team-stealing controversies |
| Discovery/browse library | VGCPastes manages 1,150+ teams in spreadsheets; no good browsable UI |
| Battle-to-report pipeline | Players manually reconstruct tournament runs; no replay integration |

---

## 5. Content Creator Partnerships with Competing Tools

- **VGCguide.com** — Wolfey, Cybertron, and Aaron Traylor co-created this 100K+ word guide (launched 2022). Educational, not a team-sharing tool, but demonstrates creator willingness to collaborate on community platforms.
- **VGCPastes + Victory Road** — formal collaboration announced on Twitter: VGCPastes aggregates pastes, Victory Road provides curated rental-code teams. Community-run, not commercially sponsored.
- **Pikalytics** — widely used team builder with usage stats; no visible creator sponsorship deals found, but integrated into multiple community workflows.
- **Champions Lab / Champions Builder** — newer entrants for Pokemon Champions 2026; no creator partnerships detected yet.
- **No exclusive sponsorship deals** found between top creators and any team-sharing tool. The space is open for a first-mover partnership.
