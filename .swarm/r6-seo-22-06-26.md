## Top 10 keyword gaps

Methodology: 8 WebSearch queries against VGC team-builder, speed-tier, OTS-generator, damage-calculator, PokéPaste-alternative, and EV-spread intent. Mapped each SERP to the strongest existing site (Pikalytics / PokéPaste / VGCpastes / Limitless / Trainer Hill / Smogon) and to where VGC Team Report's page-1 inclusion would require either new content, new metadata, or a new dynamic route. Ranked by **(intent volume × competitive defensibility)**.

The format has shifted: **Reg M-B is the live ladder** (per Pikalytics, ChampTeams.gg, Champions Lab). **Reg M-A is still the Worlds 2026 format** (Aug 28–30, San Francisco). Our pages target M-A by name; we have a stale-format risk on every page that says "Regulation M-A" without also signaling M-B awareness.

### Gap 1 — "Pokemon Champions VGC 2026" / "Reg M-B [X]" (live-format risk)

**SERP owners:** Pikalytics (`pikalytics.com/champions`, `/pokedex/gen9championsvgc2026regma`), Pokémon Zone, ChampTeams.gg.

We rank for our brand on this query but our `/champions` metadata reads "Regulation M-A". Pikalytics now renders "Reg M-B S3 Ranked Battle Data" on its `<h1>`. **The whole roster of competitor builders has flipped to M-B.** When users search "Pokemon Champions VGC 2026" today, our page reads as out-of-date even though our app supports the format. Fix: update titles/descs on `/`, `/champions`, `/explore` to acknowledge **both** M-A (Worlds) and M-B (ladder). Zero new content; pure metadata copy.

### Gap 2 — "[Restricted legendary] EV spread VGC 2026"

**Examples:** "Calyrex Shadow EV spread", "Calyrex Ice spread Reg I", "Miraidon SP spread", "Zamazenta best spread VGC".

**SERP owners:** Pikalytics species pages (`pikalytics.com/pokedex/gen9vgc2026regi/Calyrex-Shadow`), Smogon forums, VGCCorner tweets, PokeStats guides.

Identical to last cycle's Gap #1 (per-species pages) and **still unshipped**. The data exists in our DB (every public share's paste). We render Mega species pages already — extending the same template to the 12 high-volume restricted legendaries (Calyrex-Ice/Shadow, Miraidon, Koraidon, Zamazenta, Zacian, Terapagos, Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza, Lunala, Solgaleo) would harvest the largest single category of long-tail VGC search.

### Gap 3 — "VGC speed tiers 2026 / Reg M-B / Reg I"

**SERP owners:** PokeStats (`pokestats.cc/guides/vgc-speed-tiers`), Pikalytics (`pikalytics.com/speed-tiers`), Smogon resource thread, GoldenrodPress.

Every competitor has a **dedicated speed-tiers landing page**. We have a speed-tier *feature inside the report* (`SpeedTierChart`), no public landing page. The exact phrase "VGC speed tiers" appears nowhere indexable on our site. Fix: a thin `/speed-tiers` (or extend `/champions`) page that publishes the canonical Reg M-A and M-B speed benchmarks and links to the in-app calculator. Reuse existing speed-tier data; no manual upkeep beyond a yearly format refresh.

### Gap 4 — "Open Team Sheet generator" / "VGC OTS generator"

**SERP owners:** thefrontiervgc.com/teamsheet (top), dhsufi.github.io/PokemonTeamListCreator, tehdrink (VGC 2023 — stale).

Same as last cycle's Gap #3 — still unshipped, and **the SERP has weakened in our favor** (the top contender is a Wix-style static form, no team analysis, no QR). We have `OTSSheetModal` deep-linked from the share menu. A new `/ots-generator` static route with `<h1>Pokemon VGC Open Team Sheet Generator</h1>`, paste input, and the existing modal flow would be a 2-hour ship and could rank top-3 within weeks.

### Gap 5 — "VGC team builder" (head term)

**SERP owners:** Pikalytics `/team`, pokemonbuilder.com (synthetic battle-replay-stats angle), ChampTeams.gg, Champions Builder, VGC Trainer.

This is the single highest-volume intent in our category. Our `/explore` title is "Best VGC Teams 2026 — Explore Top Team Reports" — that targets *browsing*, not *building*. Our actual builder lives on `/` but the homepage `<title>` is "VGC Team Report - Build and Share Pokemon VGC Teams" — wordy and brand-leading. Fix: a metadata copy refresh on `/` that front-loads "VGC Team Builder" (e.g. `"VGC Team Builder — Build, Analyse & Share Pokemon Teams"`). Same product; better intent match.

### Gap 6 — "PokéPaste alternative" / "share Pokemon team with sprites"

**SERP owners:** crob.at (`crob.at/pokepaste-alternative` — explicit alternative-targeted landing), vrpastes.com.

crob.at has *built a landing page* with `<h1>Best PokePaste Alternative — Visual Team Sharing</h1>`. We are objectively a better PokéPaste alternative for write-up-quality sharing, but we don't compete on this query because we have no copy targeting it. Fix: a tweak to the homepage description (one sentence) explicitly comparing against PokéPaste, **plus** an FAQ item ("Is VGC Team Report a PokéPaste alternative?") — the latter would surface on the existing `FAQPageJsonLd` (cheap structured data).

### Gap 7 — "VGC team analysis" / "VGC team coach" / "type coverage"

**SERP owners:** vgccoach.pro (literal `<h1>` "Pokémon Type Chart — VGC Type Coverage"), ChampTeams.gg, StrataDex, ChartDex.

These tools all render type-coverage matrices and threat-analysis cards. We render the same (`Speed Tiers`, `Offensive`, `Defensive` slides in every report) but **only inside an authored report**, never as a standalone tool surface. Gap: no `/analyse` or `/type-coverage` page. Out of scope for a code-only fix without a new tool route, but we **can** add `"VGC type coverage"`, `"VGC team analysis"`, `"VGC matchup chart"` to the existing `/` and `/explore` keyword arrays and reference the in-report capability in descriptions.

### Gap 8 — "Pokemon damage calculator VGC"

**SERP owners:** porygonlabs.com, pikalytics.com/damage-calculator, vgccalc.com, nerd-of-now.github.io/NCP-VGC-Damage-Calculator, vgcmulticalc.com.

We have an in-report calc UI (`addCalc`, `removeCalc` in `useHomePage`) but no public `/damage-calculator` URL and no standalone calc page. This SERP is **saturated** with battle-tested calculators; competing on the head term isn't realistic without shipping a real standalone calc route. Mitigation: add "damage calculator", "damage calcs VGC" to keywords on `/` so we capture mid-funnel "VGC damage calc explanation" queries — we're well-positioned for those because every shared report is a worked example of why specific calcs justify a spread.

### Gap 9 — "VGC tournament team list" / "Regional team list"

**SERP owners:** limitlessvgc.com/teams, victoryroad.pro, smogon sample-team threads.

Our `/tournaments` page mentions Indianapolis 2026 and Worlds 2026 but doesn't have a *teams-from-this-tournament* landing per event. The shares table has tournament names — a `/tournaments/[slug]` route would render the public teams for a given tournament with an `ItemList` JSON-LD. Out of code-only scope (a new route is a real ticket), but we **can** start by enriching the existing `/tournaments` page metadata with more tournament-name keywords ("Indy Regionals teams", "Worlds 2026 teams").

### Gap 10 — "Regulation H teams" / per-regulation landing

**SERP owners:** VGCpastes, limitlessvgc, smogon (sample-team threads), Pikalytics.

Same as last cycle's Gap #2 — still unshipped. `/explore?regulation=H` works but doesn't have a static, indexable URL. Pikalytics has a *per-regulation pokedex page* at each format slug. We'd need `/format/reg-h` etc. — that's a real route, not a metadata tweak, so it stays in keyword-gap territory not "top 5 implementable".
