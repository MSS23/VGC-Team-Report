## Outreach Strategy

### What We Offer

1. **Pre-built reports of their teams** — generate a polished report before reaching out, include it in the DM
2. **Early access to new features** — if we're building something relevant (e.g., VGC-75 Mega stat comparison)
3. **Custom report themes** — if they want branded colors (future feature)
4. **Co-promotion** — feature their content on our site/socials

### What We Ask

- Mention the tool in a video (even briefly: "I made a report on pokemonvgcteamreport.com")
- Link in video description
- Optional: dedicated "How I Build Team Reports" video
