# VGC-67: DM 5 VGC Content Creators with Pre-Built Reports

**Status:** Backlog (P1)
**Goal:** Personally reach out to 5 VGC content creators with pre-built team reports of their recent teams to drive organic mentions.

## Strategy

Lead with value, not an ask. Build a report of their team before reaching out. The report itself demonstrates the tool — no pitch needed.

**How to fill this table:**
1. Search Twitter for `#VGC2026 Champions team` or `#RegulationMA`
2. Check recent Regional/IC results for top-placing players who post teams
3. Look at YouTube for recent Champions team analysis videos
4. Pick creators who actively share teams publicly (not just results)

### Selection Criteria
- Has posted a Champions format team publicly in the last 2 weeks
- Active engagement (replies to DMs/comments)
- Mix of sizes: 2 large (50K+), 2 mid (5-50K), 1 small but growing
- Prefer creators who already make team breakdown content (natural fit)

## Outreach Process

### Step 1: Find Their Team
- Check their recent tweets, YouTube videos, or Pokepaste links
- Get the Showdown paste or manually reconstruct from their content

### Step 2: Build the Report
- Paste their team into pokemonvgcteamreport.com/champions
- Add matchup notes if they mentioned any in their content
- Save and get a shareable link
- Also export a PNG screenshot

### Step 3: Send the DM

**Template:**
```
Hey [name]! Saw your [team/video] with [notable Pokemon, e.g., Mega Rayquaza + Kyogre] — sick team.

I made a tool for Champions team reports and built one for your team:
[shareable report link]

Speed tiers, EV spreads, everything in one shareable page. Free to use.

No ask here — just thought you'd think it was cool. Let me know if you want anything tweaked on the report!
```

### Step 4: Follow Up (if needed)
- Wait 5 days before following up
- Keep it short: "Hey, just bumping this — here's a quick screenshot if easier: [PNG]"
- If no response after follow-up, move on. Don't push.

## Pre-Outreach Checklist

- [ ] Champions reports are working reliably (no broken sprites, fast load)
- [ ] Share links are stable and won't break
- [ ] PNG export is clean
- [ ] Have at least 1 sample team report ready as backup demo
- [ ] Your Twitter/X account looks legitimate (not brand new, has some content)

## Timing

- Send DMs Tuesday-Thursday (higher open rates)
- Avoid tournament weekends (creators are busy/traveling)
- Coordinate with VGC-87 (Twitter campaign) — ideally DM creators 2-3 days before the public campaign so they can amplify
- Coordinate with VGC-85 (YouTube partnerships) — this is the lightweight version; escalate interested creators to the YouTube partnership track

## Success Criteria

- 5 DMs sent
- At least 2 responses (40% response rate)
- At least 1 public mention or share
- Bonus: creator starts using the tool for their own content

## Notes

- This is about relationships, not transactions. Even if they don't share, having VGC creators aware of the tool is valuable.
- Screenshot their team from their content and reference it specifically — shows you're not mass-DMing
- If a creator gives feedback, log it and act on it quickly — nothing builds goodwill like shipping their suggestion
- Each creator DM takes ~30 min of prep (finding team, building report, writing personalized message). Budget 3 hours total.
